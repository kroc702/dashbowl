DASHBOWL

Miniatures game on the theme of fantasy football. Simple, community-driven rules. Fast-paced matches full of twists.

Playtest the game Join Discord Discover the rules

Why Dashbowl?

Because there is a sweet spot between Blood Bowl and Blitz Bowl.

Fast & action-packed

Streamlined rules for non-stop action (~60 min). Fast ball movement.

Focused on touchdowns

Only touchdowns score points. Beware of turnovers, and the hexagonal grid brings a fresh tactical perspective.

And heroic actions

Special actions are managed via team decks, allowing wild imagination while keeping core rules minimal.
