To play Dashbowl, you will need a hexagonal grid board (12 x 19) with a 12-space bounce/scatter template.
You can also try playing on a Blood Bowl Sevens or Blitz Bowl pitch; in that case, use a D8 or D16 for scatters.

A D12 die.
You can also try using 2xD6 for test rolls and a D6 for scatters.

A ball and two teams (miniatures, team roster sheets, and optionally an action deck if you are testing that rule).
